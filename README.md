# 🚀 ZED'S REVENGE

**Zakardia 3101 — Rise of the Small Investor**

A 10-chapter story-driven GameFi experience set in the dystopian megacity of Zerath.

---

## 📖 Story

In the year 3101, humanity lives on the planet **Zakardia**. The only city is **Zerath** — a 500-story neon metropolis ruled by **Zorak Creed** (the Exchange Baron) and his puppet leader **Krump**.

Below the surface, in **Depth 7**, the small investors live in misery. When Zorak Creed liquidates the entire market on **October 10, 3101**, one man loses everything: his sister, his savings, his hope.

His name is **Zed**.

Armed with a homemade rocket called **VOLKAN-1**, he rises from the underground to take back what was stolen.

## 🕹️ How to Play

- **Space** or **Click/Tap** to jump
- Avoid the walls (Krump & Zorak Creed propaganda posters)
- Collect **Z Coins** (Zakardia Coin)
- Reach **150 points** to complete Chapter 1
- Every **20 points**, the drones speed up (7 speed levels total)
- Your **fuel** runs out exactly when you reach the surface
